BEFORE YOU BUY IN NEPAL

Check more than the price.

Match the exact variant

RAM, storage, processor, generation, and accessories can change a device’s price. Open the model page and check the configuration against the seller’s listing.

Ask about the local warranty

Confirm who provides service in Nepal, the warranty period, and whether the quoted price includes a VAT bill. Shop stock and service terms are not established by a reference listing.

Check the date and source

Prices can change after a source was checked. Each product page links the reference used. Ask the seller for a current quote before paying.

Understand proposed offers

The 10 laptop discounts are proposed calculations: reference price × 0.90. They require confirmation from Eakraj Mobile Shop and are labelled separately from the reference price.
