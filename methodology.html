Know what you’re comparing.

Our catalog combines sourced factual specifications with clearly labelled buying guidance.

Prices, variants, and availability

Prices are Nepal market references collected from Gadgetbyte Nepal model listings and its Nepal mobile price list on 31 October 2026. The retained Galaxy A56 and Redmi Note 14 references were checked on 30 October. Each model page links its source and lists its variants.

Reference prices are not Eakraj’s guaranteed selling prices or a live stock feed. Confirm stock, regional model, colour, warranty, taxes, included charger, and final price with us on 9707293923.

Specifications and product photographs

Each model page links its specification source. Photos are representative product photos from the linked source pages and are hosted locally for reliable loading. A photo may show a different colour or storage configuration. Source specifications may change or contain mistakes; confirm purchase-critical details with the manufacturer and exact unit.

What our “reviews” mean

The reviews area contains specification-based buying overviews written to explain the listed hardware. Eakraj has not performed hands-on testing for these listings. We do not publish invented star ratings, benchmark scores, gaming FPS, charging times, or measured battery endurance. Where a matching independent review link was found, it is provided on the model page.

Hardware class method

Classes use the listed chipset family, not a benchmark database or the selling price. They are a rough navigation aid and may not reflect thermal limits or software optimisation.

Flagship
Recognised high-end families such as Snapdragon 8 Elite / 8 Gen 2–3, Apple A17–A20, and Dimensity 9000-series.
Performance
Families such as Snapdragon 7 / 8s, Dimensity 7000–8000-series, selected Exynos 1400–2000-series, and Apple A14–A16.
Balanced
Recognised everyday families including Helio G99/G100, Snapdragon 4/6, and selected Dimensity 6000-series.
Everyday
Other or unclassified chipsets. This is a conservative label, not proof a phone is slow.
