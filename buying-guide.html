A LITTLE CLARITY GOES A LONG WAY

Find the phone that fits your life.

Start with your budget. Then focus on the things you use every day.

01 / YOUR BUDGET

Start with the total cost.

Compare the same RAM/storage variant. Ask whether the charger, accessories, local warranty, and taxes are included.

Explore by budget →

02 / PERFORMANCE

Look beyond the RAM number.

Chipset, cooling, storage, and software all affect responsiveness. Hardware classes here are estimates; use independent benchmarks for exact speed or gaming FPS.

Explore performance hardware →

03 / CAMERAS

More megapixels isn’t the whole story.

Look at actual samples in daylight and low light, stabilisation, video, ultrawide options, and selfie quality. Specifications alone cannot establish photo quality.

Read buying overviews →

04 / BATTERY

Capacity is only a starting point.

Network signal, display, apps, and software affect endurance. Check measured tests and the included charging adapter before buying.

Explore larger batteries →

05 / CONNECTIVITY

Check your network and SIM.

Confirm local 4G/5G bands, eSIM or physical SIM support, Wi-Fi, USB features, and the exact regional model.

Explore listed 5G phones →

06 / OWNERSHIP

Ask about the years ahead.

Check manufacturer update commitments, local warranty coverage, repair support, and official versus imported units. Contact the shop for exact-unit details.
